GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers.

Can 021Labs join an existing product team?

Yes. A contract engagement can focus on a defined feature, mobile engineering work or a delivery milestone. The scope and collaboration style are agreed around your team.

Can you work with a product that is already live?

Yes. The first step is to understand the current architecture, user feedback and the outcome you want. From there, we can identify a practical, bounded next step.

What should I include in a project enquiry?

A short description of the product, who it is for, what is currently difficult and any timing or technical constraints is enough to start the conversation.
