SERVICE ENQUIRY — 03

Help us understand the equipment and the job.

Send whatever information is available. A clear starting point helps Al Syed Traders review the requirement and identify what needs to be confirmed next.

01

Equipment identity

Tell us what needs attention and include the nameplate details if you can.

  • Panel type: distribution, control, protection, or other
  • PT transformer make, model, voltage ratio, and quantity
  • Approximate age or installation date, if known
02

Work required

Describe the requested service or the issue in plain terms.

  • Installation, inspection, maintenance, or troubleshooting
  • What is happening, when it began, and whether it is recurring
  • Any previous repair or recent change to the equipment
03

Site and access

Basic site context helps establish whether a visit or more information is needed.

  • City and facility type
  • Indoor or outdoor installation and general site conditions
  • Access, outage, permit, or safety constraints to plan around
04

Helpful attachments

Share only what is available; clear photos often answer the first questions.

  • Nameplate and overall equipment photos
  • Panel single-line diagram or relevant drawings
  • Readings, fault indications, or previous service notes
