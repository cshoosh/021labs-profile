ENQUIRY GUIDE · 01

What to include in your message.

Start with the information you have. Photos, drawings and records are helpful, but you can begin with a short description.

01

Equipment

HT panel, transformer or PT transformer; make/model and nameplate details if known.

02

Service requirement

Repair, maintenance, dehydration or another requested service. Describe the symptoms and when they began.

03

Site context

Facility name, city, site access process, operating constraints and a suitable point of contact.

04

Helpful documents

Equipment photos, recent test records, drawings, maintenance notes or previous service history.

For safety, do not send passwords, access credentials or sensitive security details. Do not open or approach energized equipment; site safety and isolation must be coordinated by authorized personnel.
